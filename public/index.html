    How the grade works
    
      
    Every hour starts at 100. Points come off for each thing known to degrade GNSS positioning. Penalties scale with your geomagnetic latitude () and your work profile. The day grade blends the average and the worst hour inside your work hours (60/40), so one bad afternoon still shows.
